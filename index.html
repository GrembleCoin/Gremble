COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ.

The basics about Gremble in one place.

01 What is Gremble? +

Gremble is a memecoin project built around a character, a story and a community. The goal is to build something people enjoy being part of, not just another token.

02 What blockchain will Gremble use? +

GrembleCoin is planned for Solana. Official links and the contract address will only be shared through official Gremble channels.

03 Where can I buy Gremble? +

Trading links are not active yet. When they are available, the BUY GREMBLE button at the top of the site will point to the official locations.

04 How can I join the community? +

Join the Gremble Telegram, follow Gremble on X and take part in discussions, contests, games and community events.

05 What are the future plans? +

The plan is to keep growing the community, continue the Gremble story, develop community activities and games, launch GrembleCoin and later explore merchandise and other opportunities.
