THE BEGINNING

A WORLD THAT
NEEDED CHANGE.

Gremble came from the upper class. His family built its wealth in the previous world through weapons manufacturing and sales.

After a devastating war the world changed. Only a fraction of the population remained. Old systems survived but new opportunities appeared.

Gremble wanted something different. Instead of simply following the path prepared for him, he wanted to build something of his own.
